# 0.9.0-beta.1

This is the first public local couch co-op beta for the Gang Beasts Sandevistan mod.

Each local player presses **R3** to activate their own Sandevistan. Their active window lasts three real seconds and their six-second cooldown starts when their own activation ends. Activations can overlap: the world stays at 20% speed until the last active player ends, while each active Beast receives scoped compensation.

Requires Xbox PC Gang Beasts 1.28 and MelonLoader 0.7.3. Local/offline matches only; the mod rejects remote human players. This beta does not include automated-test DLLs.

Please report map, controller, physics, collision, grab, recovery and multiplayer issues with reproducible steps and a sanitised `MelonLoader/Latest.log`.
